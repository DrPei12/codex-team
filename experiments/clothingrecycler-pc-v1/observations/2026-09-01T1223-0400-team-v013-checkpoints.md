## 0.1.3 已实施改进

### Requirement coverage lattice

- manifest required `requirements`：id/statement、contract invariant、`change`或`verification-only`、owner lanes、concrete owned paths、Gate ids、reviewer lane。
- 每条contract invariant必须exact覆盖；change path必须在列出的owner中恰有一个可写owner，forbidden deny优先；verification-only不声明可写path但仍绑定owner/Gate/reviewer。
- lane required `requirement_ids`与`does_not_cover`；owner和reviewer不得遗漏关联requirement。

### Worker backbrief

- `team-run prepare`生成hash-bound backbrief template与canonical `worker_backbrief_argv`。
- Backbrief绑定passed preflight、dispatch、brief、requirements、ownership、Gate、does-not-cover、first bounded action和planned evidence。
- Exact scope且无未解决假设/问题时`passed`；有assumption/open question时`needs-input`；requirement/ownership/Gate丢失或篡改时`failed`。
- Receipt与input bytes/hash、preflight和dispatch绑定；exclusive、不可覆盖。

### Stage checkpoint 与 progress policy

- manifest required checkpoints绑定after/before lanes、requirements、acceptance owner、evidence与accepted-state resume。
- lane required manifest-specific heartbeat/max-turn policy；skill没有通用时间常数，max-turn不得短于heartbeat，limit action固定`checkpoint-stop`。
- status facts新增checkpoint state和phase/material-progress事实。
- 依赖accepted但checkpoint pending时before lane为`waiting-checkpoint`；active lane缺backbrief、缺完整progress、heartbeat/turn超限分别显示`backbrief-required`/`checkpoint-required`。
- 所有动作仍为非破坏性状态建议；没有自动interrupt、message、archive、redispatch或cleanup。
